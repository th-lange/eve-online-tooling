Cargo efficiency

Type an item name, pick it, and the plugin scores its cargo density and ISK-per-m³ value.

How it's scored. Two cargo-efficiency figures: density = round(1000 ÷ volume m³) (volume only — a smaller item packs tighter) and the price-aware ISK/m³ = Jita sell price ÷ volume — how much ISK of value each m³ of cargo carries, so the higher it is the more an item is worth hauling. Density comes from the evaluate export; the price side is a separate price export needing the market:read grant and live data, so you still get density without it. The evaluated N× counter is kept in the plugin's own sandboxed storage.
