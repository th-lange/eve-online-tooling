Cargo efficiency

Type an item name, pick it, and the plugin scores its cargo density.

How it's scored. Cargo efficiency = round(1000 ÷ packaged volume in m³). A smaller item scores higher — more units fit per m³ of cargo hold (e.g. a 0.01 m³ item scores 100 000; a 10 m³ item scores 100). It's a deterministic density proxy, not an ISK figure. The evaluated N× counter is how many times this plugin has scored anything, kept in its own sandboxed storage.
